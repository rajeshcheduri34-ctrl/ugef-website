Who we are

A shared commitment to a greener future.

Unity Green Environmental Forum (UGEF) is an environmental initiative of Vidhya Margha Educational Society, based in Yerragondapalem, Andhra Pradesh. The Forum aims to bring together students, educators, researchers, volunteers and communities to support environmental protection and sustainable development.

Through awareness, education, research, partnerships and community-based programmes, UGEF seeks to encourage practical environmental action and responsible stewardship of natural resources.

Our objectives

Turning environmental values into action.

The Forum's proposed objectives span conservation, education, research and community development.

✓Promote environmental protection, ecological balance and sustainable development.
✓Conserve forests, wetlands, rivers, lakes, groundwater and other natural resources.
✓Support tree plantation, afforestation and green-belt development initiatives.
✓Encourage biodiversity conservation and protection of natural habitats.
✓Build awareness about air, water, soil and noise pollution.
✓Organise environmental education, seminars, workshops and public campaigns.
✓Engage schools, colleges, youth, women, farmers and local communities.
✓Promote waste segregation, recycling, reuse and responsible resource recovery.
✓Encourage plastic reduction, composting and scientific waste management.
✓Promote safe, authorised channels for e-waste and battery recycling.
✓Support rainwater harvesting, groundwater recharge and water-body restoration.
✓Build awareness of climate change, resilience and greenhouse-gas reduction.
✓Encourage renewable energy, energy efficiency and sustainable consumption.
✓Promote environmental research, surveys, monitoring and innovation.
✓Collaborate with educational institutions, research bodies and public agencies.
✓Share environmental knowledge through reports, publications and learning resources.
✓Encourage sustainable agriculture, soil conservation and eco-friendly livelihoods.
✓Support sanitation, clean-community initiatives and environmental health awareness.

Knowledge & research

Science-informed environmental action.

UGEF aims to encourage evidence-based understanding of environmental challenges and share useful knowledge with communities and institutions.

Areas for future collaboration

  • Environmental quality awareness and community monitoring
  • Water, soil and waste management studies
  • Resource recovery and circular-economy approaches
  • Climate resilience and sustainable livelihoods
  • Student projects, workshops and knowledge publications

Research activities and collaborations will be undertaken subject to resources, approvals and appropriate institutional partnerships.
